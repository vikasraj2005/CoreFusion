About

CoreFusion is a dynamic two-day technical extravaganza that brings together the brilliance of five core engineering departments.

  • Robotics And Automation Engineering
  • Electronics And Communication Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electrical And Electronics Engineering
  • Electronics And Instrumentation Engineering

It brings together innovation, technology, and creativity through exciting competitions, workshops, and hands-on experiences. CoreFusion is a platform for students to explore ideas, showcase skills, and connect with emerging trends in engineering.
